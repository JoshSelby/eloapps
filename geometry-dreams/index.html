A YouTube channel by ELO Apps

Geometry Dreams publishes original pattern art. The Shorts show a pattern being built from a single line, ray or point until the full design appears. The long videos are hours of slowly drifting fractals with soothing ambient sound, made for sleep, study and calm. Every visual and sound is original.

Watch on YouTube: @geometry-dreams

Geometry Dreams Uploader

Geometry Dreams Uploader is a private tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer to publish the channel's videos. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the Geometry Dreams channel, with their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, and to confirm it is signed in to that channel. It is not offered to anyone else and does not access other people's accounts or data.
