How the math works

The Gold Star membership is $65 a year and Executive is $130, so the upgrade costs $65. Executive pays a 2% reward on qualifying purchases, up to $1,250 a year.

$65 ÷ 2% = $3,250. If you spend more than about $3,250 a year ($271 a month) on things that qualify, the reward more than covers the upgrade. Below that, you'd be paying for a reward you don't earn back.

What doesn't count

Costco's member terms exclude these from the 2% reward:

  • Gas at Costco gas stations (the Costco Anywhere Visa's 5% on gas is a separate card reward)
  • Food court purchases
  • Sales tax, fees and deposits
  • Tobacco, and alcohol in many states
  • Some services such as eye exams, tire installation and car purchases

Other things to weigh

  • Executive members also get extra benefits on some Costco services, which can be worth more than the 2% if you use them.
  • The reward arrives once a year as a certificate you spend at Costco.
  • You can usually downgrade back to Gold Star if it doesn't pay off.
